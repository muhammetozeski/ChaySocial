# Muhurlu Cekmece

Kategori: Yazma | Efor: M | Rank: 1

## Ne
Bitmemis yaziyi yazarin kendi anahtariyla muhurleyip saklayan bir cekmece. Tamamen ClippingData/ClippingService kalibi.

1) Yeni `MainProject/DataModels/DraftData.cs` — `sealed record DraftData : IStoredDocument<DraftData>`, `CollectionName => "drafts"`. Alanlar: DraftId, WriterAddress, Encapsulation, Nonce, Ciphertext, UpdatedAtUnixMs (hepsi required). `Id => new DocumentId<DraftData>(DraftId)`. Sabit modelin uzerinde durur, ClippingData.ClippingIdBytes'in aynadigi gibi: `public const int DraftIdBytes = 12`. Sorgu alanlari: `static readonly DocumentField<DraftData> WriterField = new(nameof(WriterAddress), draft => draft.WriterAddress)` ve `UpdatedAtField = new(nameof(UpdatedAtUnixMs), draft => draft.UpdatedAtUnixMs)`.

2) Yeni `MainProject/Services/DraftService.cs` — ClippingService.cs satir satir sablon:
- `public readonly record struct KeptDraft(string DraftId, string Text, string Title, string LongBody, long UpdatedAtUnixMs)`.
- Servis sabitleri (ClippingService'teki ShelfPageSize/KeptTimeBucketMs'in karsiliklari): `public const int DrawerPageSize = 100;`, `const long UpdatedTimeBucketMs = 60_000;`, `static readonly byte[] DraftEnvelopeDomain = "ChaySocial/Draft/v1"u8.ToArray();`.
- `SaveAsync(PrivateIdentity writer, string draftId, string text, string title, string longBody)`: uc alan da bostan ibaretse null doner; PostData.MaximumTextLength (500), MaximumTitleLength (120), MaximumLongBodyLength (20_000) asilirsa reddeder. draftId bos ise `Base32.Encode(RandomSource.Next(DraftData.DraftIdBytes))` ile yeni id uretir, dolu ise ayni id'ye yazar — boylece "tekrar sakla" ayni belgeyi degistirir, kopya yigmaz. Muhurleme: `AppCryptography.Identities.EncapsulateTo(writer.Public)`, `RandomSource.Next(AppCryptography.Cipher.NonceSize)`, `EnvelopePadding.Pad(WriteBody(...))`, `AppCryptography.Cipher.Encrypt(paddedBody, secret.SharedSecret, nonce, BuildAssociatedData(draftId, writer.Public.Address, roundedUpdatedAt))`. Belgeye yazilan zaman `exact - (exact % UpdatedTimeBucketMs)`; gercek an muhurun icinde.
- `WriteBody`: TranscriptWriter ile `WriteInt64(exactUpdatedAt)`, `WriteText(text)`, `WriteText(title)`, `WriteText(longBody)` — adli degil konumlu, ClippingService.WriteBody'nin belgeledigi sebeple: adli alan bos degeri hic yazmiyor ve bos baslik burada normal durum, konumlar kayardi.
- `ReadDrawerAsync(PrivateIdentity writer, int limit = DrawerPageSize)`: `new DocumentQuery<DraftData>().WithMatch(DraftData.WriterField, writer.Public.Address).WithSort(DraftData.UpdatedAtField, descending: true).WithLimit(limit)`, sonra her satir icin TryOpen; acilmayan satir atlanir, atilmaz.
- `TryOpen`: adres denetimi, uc alan icin TryFromBase64, `encapsulation.Length != AppCryptography.KeyExchange.EncapsulationSize` on denetimi, `writer.Decapsulate`, `AppCryptography.Cipher.TryDecrypt`, `EnvelopePadding.TryUnpad`, sonra ReadBody: TranscriptReader ile `TryReadBytes` (8 bayt + `BinaryPrimitives.ReadInt64BigEndian`) ve uc `TryReadText`. (TranscriptReader'da TryReadInt64 yok, ClippingService.ReadBody de boyle yapiyor.)
- `DiscardAsync(PrivateIdentity writer, string draftId)`: ReadAsync, WriterAddress sahiplik denetimi, DeleteAsync — ClippingService.ForgetAsync'in aynisi.

3) Yeni `MainProject/UI/Elements/Social/DraftDrawer.razor` + `DraftDrawerCss.razor`. PostComposer'in icine gomulmez; MediaPicker, PollComposer, DrawingPicker, LongFormComposer nasil ayri bilesen ise bu da oyle. Parametreler: Text/TextChanged, Title/TitleChanged, LongBody/LongBodyChanged, IsDisabled. Imzalayiciyi `SessionService.Current` uzerinden kendisi alir (DrawingPicker'in MediaService'e kendi ulasmasi gibi). Panel `<AppOverlay>` icinde, DrawingPicker'in backdrop+panel bicimiyle; yuzey `AppStyles.BuildAcrylicStyle(AcrylicLevel.Strong, AppMeasures.Blur.Heavy)`. "Keep for later" komposerdekini muhurler; panel ReadDrawerAsync satirlarini ilk satiri ve `RelativeTimeFormatter.Format(UpdatedAtUnixMs)` ile listeler; bir satira dokunmak uc Changed geri cagrisi uzerinden komposere geri yazar; satirin yaninda DiscardAsync.

4) `MainProject/UI/Elements/Social/PostComposer.razor`: `post-composer-tools` satirina `<DrawingPicker>` yanina tek satir `<DraftDrawer ... />`. Baska hicbir yer degismez; metni Wall.razor.cs sahiplenmeye devam eder.

5) Taslaga ek/medya, anket ve alinti girmez — sahipsiz blob kalmasin ve muhurun icinden hicbir blob'a isaret cikmasin diye.

DraftDrawerCss'teki her px AppMeasures (Space/Radius/Blur), her renk AppColors'tan gelir; dosyada duz sayi yok.

## Neden
Komposerdeki yazi bir sayfa alani: Wall.razor.cs'te ComposerText (176), ComposerTitle (285), ComposerLongBody (288). Bir karta dokunmak, alt menuden baska yere gecmek ya da sayfayi yenilemek hepsini siliyor; 20 000 karakterlik uzun yazi zaten tek oturumda bitmeyen sey. Cekmece bunu tarayici deposuna duz metin olarak birakmadan cozer: yazarin kendi anahtariyla muhurlendigi icin hesap hangi cihazda acilirsa oraya gelir, sunucu ise yalnizca bir adresin taslak tuttugunu gorur, ne yazdigini degil.
